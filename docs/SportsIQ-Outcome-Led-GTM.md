# Sports-IQ Outcome-Led GTM

## The Promise

**Know every fan. Improve every game-day journey. Turn the right moments into measurable value.**

Sports-IQ helps sports organizations understand what each fan needs in the moment, choose the next best action, and learn from every interaction. That action may be a relevant offer, useful guidance, a loyalty benefit, or no interruption at all.

The proposition is not "send more promotions." It is to create a connected fan experience that improves convenience, protects the live moment, grows fan value, and gives commercial and venue teams measurable evidence of what works.

## Executive Narrative

Most sports organizations already have valuable fan data, but it is fragmented across ticketing, loyalty, mobile apps, point of sale, parking, venue operations, and game systems. That fragmentation makes it difficult to recognize a fan's current context or respond while the moment still matters.

Sports-IQ creates a shared understanding of the fan and the game-day journey. It combines what is happening now with what the organization already knows, then uses trusted venue, policy, loyalty, weather, and journey knowledge to recommend a relevant and feasible next action.

This creates a simple value loop:

1. **Understand** the fan, their history, and their current situation.
2. **Decide** whether assistance, an offer, or silence is the best experience.
3. **Act** while the opportunity is still relevant.
4. **Measure** the fan and commercial outcome.
5. **Learn** what should happen differently next time.

## Outcome Plays

### 1. Know Your Fans and Create Hyper-Personalized Offers

**Business outcome:** Increase spend and conversion by making each offer relevant to the fan, the game, and the moment.

Instead of sending the same halftime promotion to everyone, recognize that one fan usually buys food in the second quarter, another responds to merchandise after a win, and another should not receive an offer because they recently redeemed one.

#### Personalized Offer Possibilities

- Match live behavior with loyalty tier, purchase patterns, preferences, seat location, and offer history.
- Detect when a fan is behind their normal purchase or engagement pattern.
- Select an offer the fan is likely to value and can realistically use.
- Personalize timing, category, value, message, and delivery channel.
- Attribute redemption and revenue to the decision that created it.

#### Personalized Offer Prerequisites

- Unify the fan data estate across live signals and analytical history.
- Resolve consented fan identity consistently across ticketing, app, loyalty, and POS data.
- Connect the current fan to the current game using both fan and game identifiers.
- Capture offer delivery, redemption, transaction, and non-response events.
- Establish control groups to distinguish incremental value from sales that would have happened anyway.

#### Personalized Offer Measures

- Incremental revenue and contribution margin per addressable fan
- Conversion and redemption lift versus a control group
- Average transaction value
- Cost per incremental redemption
- Offer fatigue and opt-out rate

**Buyer conversation:** Chief Revenue Officer, Chief Marketing Officer, Head of Loyalty

### 2. Protect the Live Moment While Growing Revenue

**Business outcome:** Increase commercial effectiveness without degrading the experience fans came to enjoy.

The next best action is sometimes no action. A fan watching a critical play, celebrating a score, or showing high engagement should not be interrupted. Sports-IQ identifies natural pauses and low-friction moments, then suppresses commercial messages during peak excitement.

#### Moment Protection Possibilities

- Recognize sport-specific moments such as Red Zone drives, clutch time, power plays, VAR reviews, and intermissions.
- Reserve commercial engagement for timeouts, breaks, arrival windows, and other natural pauses.
- Apply loyalty-aware contact limits, per-game budget controls, and responsible-offer policies.
- Coordinate service messages and promotions so they do not compete for attention.

#### Moment Protection Prerequisites

- Stream reliable game-state and fan-engagement signals.
- Define sport-specific moments when outreach is allowed, discouraged, or prohibited.
- Agree on frequency, eligibility, budget, alcohol, and safety policies.
- Monitor decision latency so an approved action does not arrive after the moment has passed.

#### Moment Protection Measures

- Revenue or margin per message sent
- Conversion by game moment
- Notification dismissal and opt-out rates
- Engagement recovery after intervention
- Decision-to-delivery latency

**Buyer conversation:** Chief Experience Officer, Chief Revenue Officer, Digital Product Leader

### 3. Make Every Recommendation Contextually Relevant and Operationally Feasible

**Business outcome:** Replace generic recommendations with actions grounded in the realities of the venue.

A relevant offer is still a poor experience if the item is unavailable, the concession is too far away, the queue is too long, or the fan is seated in an exposed section during bad weather. Sports-IQ combines fan intent with venue and operational knowledge so recommendations can be acted on.

#### Contextual Recommendation Possibilities

- Recommend the nearest suitable concession or merchandise location.
- Account for walk time, venue level, accessibility, weather exposure, menu, and preparation time.
- Match loyalty benefits to the fan's entitlement and current context.
- Adapt recommendations when a zone is closed, congested, or unable to fulfill demand.
- Use weather to prioritize mobile ordering, seat delivery, hot beverages, rain gear, or sheltered routes.

#### Contextual Recommendation Prerequisites

- Curate trusted venue guides, concession maps, menus, loyalty rules, and operating policies.
- Connect current weather and operational status to the decision context.
- Assign content ownership and freshness standards to venue and loyalty teams.
- Require grounded answers and preserve a trace to the knowledge used.

#### Contextual Recommendation Measures

- Offer fulfillment and redemption rate
- Abandoned order rate
- Average walk and wait time
- Recommendation accuracy
- Grounded-response and policy-compliance rate

**Buyer conversation:** Chief Operating Officer, Venue Operations Leader, Head of Guest Experience

### 4. Remove Friction From Doorstep to Seat and Back Again

**Business outcome:** Make game day easier before the fan enters the venue and after the event ends.

The fan experience starts before arrival. Sports-IQ can evolve from in-venue engagement into a journey companion that helps fans plan when to leave, choose a route, find parking, reach the right gate, navigate the venue, and plan the trip home.

#### Connected Journey Possibilities

- Combine ticket, venue, weather, traffic, transit, and parking context in one fan journey.
- Recommend parking based on the fan's gate, accessibility needs, expected arrival, and availability.
- Provide route and departure guidance that adapts to current conditions.
- Guide fans from parking or transit to the right entrance and onward to their seats.
- Recommend the least-friction return route as crowds leave the venue.
- Trigger pre-game services and loyalty benefits without forcing fans to search across apps.

#### Connected Journey Prerequisites

- Connect trusted web context for traffic, transit, road closures, and parking availability.
- Maintain authoritative venue, gate, accessibility, and parking knowledge.
- Obtain fan consent for location-aware assistance and clearly communicate data use.
- Define handoffs to mapping, parking, ticketing, and team-app experiences.
- Design fallback guidance when live external data is unavailable.

#### Connected Journey Measures

- On-time arrival and gate-entry rate
- Parking-assistance completion
- Time from venue perimeter to seat
- Game-day support contacts
- Fan satisfaction and app retention
- Pre-game service and parking revenue

**Buyer conversation:** Chief Operating Officer, Chief Experience Officer, Head of Digital

### 5. Turn Every Interaction Into a Learning Loop

**Business outcome:** Build a measurable system that improves fan decisions over time instead of running disconnected campaigns.

Each recommendation should produce evidence: Was it delivered? Did the fan engage? Was there a transaction? Did it improve the experience or create fatigue? Sports-IQ connects actions to outcomes so teams can continuously improve targeting, policies, and investment.

#### Learning Loop Possibilities

- Link recommendations to delivery, response, redemption, and transaction outcomes.
- Compare performance by segment, offer, venue zone, channel, and game moment.
- Identify which interventions create incremental margin rather than attributed gross sales.
- Update fan preferences and future decision policies from observed behavior.
- Give commercial, experience, and operations teams a shared view of performance.

#### Learning Loop Prerequisites

- Create a common measurement model across fan, game, offer, and transaction events.
- Preserve correlation identifiers from decision through fulfillment.
- Establish holdout groups and experimentation governance.
- Separate gross revenue, incremental revenue, contribution margin, and program cost.

#### Learning Loop Measures

- Incremental contribution margin versus control
- Attributable redemption rate
- Decision precision and recommendation acceptance
- Repeat engagement across games
- Fan lifetime value and retention indicators
- Cost-to-value ratio

**Buyer conversation:** Chief Data Officer, Chief Revenue Officer, Finance Leader

---

### 6. Give Teams a Shared Game-Day Operating Picture

**Business outcome:** Help revenue, guest experience, loyalty, and venue operations act from the same current understanding.

The same intelligence used for fan decisions can answer operational questions: Which sections show declining engagement? Which concession zones are active? Where is revenue at risk? Which fans are eligible for a loyalty intervention? This reduces manual analysis and shortens the distance between insight and action.

#### Operating Picture Possibilities

- Ask business questions in natural language across live and historical context.
- Detect emerging demand, congestion, service, or engagement patterns during the event.
- Give human operators context before approving high-value or sensitive actions.
- Create one measure of performance from live operations through commercial outcomes.

#### Operating Picture Prerequisites

- Standardize business entities and definitions across fan, game, venue, offer, and purchase data.
- Apply role-based access and protect personal information.
- Publish trusted measures and authoritative sources.
- Capture operator decisions as part of the learning loop.

#### Operating Picture Measures

- Time from question to decision
- Manual analysis hours avoided
- Intervention completion rate
- Operational response time
- Adoption by revenue and venue teams

**Buyer conversation:** Chief Operating Officer, Chief Data Officer, Revenue Operations Leader

## Recommended Maturity Journey

### Stage 1: See the Fan

Create a consented, measurable fan view across identity, attendance, loyalty, purchase, and interaction data.

**Exit criteria:** The organization can connect an addressable fan, their current game session, a delivered action, and the resulting outcome.

### Stage 2: Prove One Valuable Moment

Select one observable journey, such as a concession offer during a timeout, and compare it with a control group.

**Exit criteria:** The pilot demonstrates incremental margin while staying within experience, policy, and latency guardrails.

### Stage 3: Make the Action Context-Aware

Add game state, venue location, loyalty rules, operational guides, availability, and weather so each recommendation is timely and feasible.

**Exit criteria:** Recommendations meet relevance, fulfillment, grounding, and fan-experience targets.

### Stage 4: Connect the Full Game-Day Journey

Extend assistance to departure planning, parking, entry, in-venue navigation, and the return trip.

**Exit criteria:** Fans receive coordinated assistance across the journey without channel conflict or excess messaging.

### Stage 5: Scale the Learning System

Expand across segments, categories, games, venues, and sports using governed experiments and shared economics.

**Exit criteria:** Repeatable incremental contribution margin and experience improvements justify broader investment.

## Discovery Questions

### Business Value

- Which game-day revenue streams have the largest gap between fan intent and conversion?
- Where do generic campaigns create waste, low response, or fan fatigue?
- Which fan journeys have enough volume and measurable outcomes for a controlled pilot?
- How does the organization currently distinguish incremental sales from attributed sales?

### Fan Experience

- Where do fans experience the most friction before, during, and after an event?
- Which moments should never be interrupted by a commercial message?
- Which loyalty benefits are difficult for fans to discover or redeem?
- What consent and communication preferences are already captured?

### Data and Operations

- Can ticketing, app, loyalty, POS, and parking activity be resolved to the same consented fan?
- How quickly are game, transaction, queue, and inventory signals available?
- Who owns venue guides, menu data, parking information, loyalty rules, and freshness?
- Can an offer be traced from decision through delivery, redemption, and transaction?

### Scale and Governance

- Which policies require human approval or deterministic enforcement?
- What are the acceptable latency, failure, and fallback thresholds?
- Which metrics must improve before expanding to another venue or sport?
- How will commercial value and fan trust be reviewed together?

## 60-Second Executive Pitch

Sports organizations have only a few seconds to act on what is happening during a live event, but fan, transaction, venue, and game information is usually fragmented. Sports-IQ connects those signals with each fan's loyalty, spending, purchase, and response history to determine whether the next best action is useful guidance, a relevant offer, or no interruption at all.

It protects peak moments by suppressing outreach when fans are highly engaged. At the right moments, it can recommend an offer grounded in real venue constraints, help a fan find the best concession, adapt to weather, or guide their route to the stadium, parking, gate, seat, and trip home. Every action can be connected to a measurable response, creating a learning loop across revenue, experience, and operations.

The recommended entry point is a controlled pilot around one observable fan journey, measured for incremental contribution margin, fan response, and decision speed. Once proven, the same foundation can expand across the complete game-day experience.

## Value Hypothesis and Claim Discipline

The current professional football scenario model assumes:

- 68,000 stadium capacity
- 35% app adoption, producing 23,800 addressable fans
- $22.84 potential uplift per addressable fan per game across modeled offer categories
- Approximately $543,000 potential gross revenue uplift per game
- Approximately $4.3 million potential gross revenue uplift across eight home games

These figures are **modeled scenario outputs, not measured customer results**. They should be presented as a value hypothesis to test through a controlled pilot. Customer-facing business cases should show assumptions and separately report gross revenue, incremental revenue, contribution margin, program cost, and fan-experience guardrails.

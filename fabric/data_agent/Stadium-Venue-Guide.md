# Harbor Center — Stadium Venue Guide

**Home of the Harbor City Comets (professional football)**
**Stadium ID:** STD_KC_ARROWHEAD
**Capacity:** 76,416
**Surface:** Natural Grass
**Configuration:** Open-air with retractable dome capability

---

## 1. Stadium Overview

Harbor Center is a modern professional football venue located in Kansas City, hosting 8 regular-season home games plus potential playoff contests. The stadium features five seating levels, 24 concession zones, and 12 merchandise stands distributed to minimize fan walk times during TV timeouts and other intervention windows.

**Key Dimensions for Sports-IQ:**
- 100 named sections across 6 seating levels
- 24 concession zones operated by Aramark, Levy Restaurants, and Delaware North
- 12 merchandise stand locations with real-time inventory tracking
- Average concession walk time ranges from 0.6 minutes (Suite level) to 9.0 minutes (End Zone level)

## 3. Concession Zones

Harbor Center operates 24 concession zones (CZ_01 through CZ_24) staffed by three food service operators. Each zone serves the same core menu with minor price variations by operator.

### 3.1 Concession Zone Operator Map

| Zones | Operator | Specialty Note |
|-------|----------|---------------|
| CZ_01, CZ_03, CZ_08, CZ_09 | Aramark | Largest operator footprint. Known for BBQ Brisket Sandwich (signature item). Consistent prep times. |
| CZ_02, CZ_05, CZ_07 | Levy Restaurants | Premium food service. Signature Nachos and Craft Beer selection. Fast beverage service (< 1 min prep for beer). |

## 5. Weather Impact Guide

Harbor Center is an open-air venue for most games. Weather significantly affects concession and merchandise purchasing behavior.

| Temperature Range | Behavioral Impact | Offer Strategy |
|-------------------|-------------------|----------------|
| > 75°F | Cold beverages peak; food demand moderate | Push cold drinks, ice cream if available |
| 50–75°F | Balanced demand across all categories | Standard offer rotation |
| 35–50°F | Hot food and hot beverages increase 40% | Prioritize BBQ Brisket, Hot Cocoa, Pretzel |
| < 35°F | Hot beverages dominate; fan dwell time drops | Aggressive Hot Cocoa offers; hoodie/blanket merch offers; shorten offer windows |
| Precipitation | Concession walks discouraged | Emphasize mobile ordering and seat delivery |
